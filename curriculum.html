How I run a project

Scope first

Every book starts from its curriculum source: what each lesson covers, what students already know, and where the boundaries sit. That keeps writers aligned before a word is written.

One house style

I set notation and style decisions once, record them, and apply them book-wide, so a team of writers produces content that reads as one voice.

Review in rounds

Chapters move through authoring, review and edits with clear hand-offs in ClickUp. Reviewer comments are applied and logged, so nothing is lost between rounds.

Accuracy checks

Proofing and mechanical reviews, answer-key checks and validation scripts catch errors before students see them.

Train the team

I write guidelines, train new builders and writers, and stay available as the person they can ask.

AI where it helps

I use Claude daily, alongside OpenAI and Grok, to draft chapters from a curriculum source and to build review tools, always paired with human review.
