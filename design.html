Client work

Glimmercat Education: Sure Shot Novel Studies

For a Canadian education publisher, I redesigned its Sure Shot Novel Studies workbooks, covers and inside pages, moving from one shared black-and-white template to a watercolour cover for each book.

→

I kept the publisher’s brand (name, logo and cat mascot) and built a reusable layout: the same title block and fonts on every cover, with an illustration and colour palette chosen to match each story.
