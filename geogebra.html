Interactive maths content designer

Digital Content Writer at Mathspace, January 2020 to October 2026

I design and build GeoGebra applets and investigations where students explore a maths idea and show what they understand, not just enter an answer. At Mathspace, a global edtech company that creates adaptive online maths textbooks, that came to about 450 applets for Years 3 to 12 across Australian, US and Canadian courses.

I led GeoGebra development for the first edition of Mathspace’s Australian textbooks and earlier books: I created the applet design system, trained more than 10 builders (including one to take over design QA) and checked every applet before release. I still do design QA for the team.

Selected applets

Interactive GeoGebra work for Mathspace lessons and investigations. Each applet loads when you ask for it, so the page stays fast.

Furious Avians, Level 4

Public GeoGebra investigation, 2021

  • A game-style investigation: students use sliders to build a quadratic, then fire the bird to see whether their parabola hits the target.
  • I was asked to redesign an existing applet and fix its errors. I created all the image assets from scratch in Figma, debugged the applet, and resized it to add a second graphics panel so the sliders and labels sit beside the game instead of on top of it.
  • Restyled it to Mathspace’s colours, fonts and line weights, and matched the controls to the other levels in the series.
  • Flagged that the equation and its solution used different number formats (decimals and fractions), so students wouldn’t be confused.

Simulating compound events

Australian Curriculum investigation, 2024 Built from scratch

  • The writer could find simulations for a coin or a die on their own, but not both together, so I built a combined compound-event simulator from scratch.
  • I wrote the randomisation mechanics, laid out every component and tested it so it runs error-free.
  • Added animated coin flips and die rolls, a running counter and a reset button, so students can run many trials and compare their results with theory.
  • The only outside help was a designer’s coin image; the rest of the applet is my work.

Linear solutions

Australian Curriculum investigation, 2023 Built from scratch

  • Each round generates a new linear equation, using an algorithm I wrote so every equation always has whole-number solutions.
  • Students find solutions by placing points, and each point gives instant feedback.
  • The line is revealed only after three different solutions, so students can’t guess their way through. The reveal is earned by showing they understand what a solution to an equation is.
“Yes! I love this! Thanks so much for doing this so quickly!”
Sarah Butterworth, curriculum writer

Properties of real numbers

Virginia SOL student lesson, 2024

  • An applet for the commutative property: students change the order of the numbers and see that the result stays the same.
  • Inspired by another GeoGebra applet, which I used as a starting idea and rebuilt in Mathspace’s applet style.
  • I added a toggle so students switch between addition and multiplication in one applet, instead of using two separate tools.
“Teachers are going to love this.”
Victoria Lowery, Virginia project lead

Comparing data sets

Australian Curriculum student lesson, 2024

  • An applet for comparing two data sets using histograms and frequency polygons.
  • The brief was to create something like a reference applet. I rebuilt how the histograms and frequency polygons are constructed so they display the data accurately.
  • The result lets students compare the shape, centre and spread of two data sets side by side.
“I think you nailed it.”
Elisha Templin, reviewer

Congruent, similar or neither?

Australian Curriculum investigation, 2023

  • Rebuilt from the ground up when the original applet could not show similarity at all.
  • Sixteen scenarios, one for each combination of given sides and angles, in almost 500 components.
  • Added a right-angle toggle, the obtuse-angle case where two triangles are possible, and a dropdown that gives students room to work.

Parallel and perpendicular lines

Virginia SOL student lesson, 2024 Built from scratch

  • Combined two separate lessons’ ideas into one applet so students compare parallel and perpendicular slopes side by side.
  • Chose input boxes over dropdowns for slope and intercept, so students type values rather than pick them.
“This looks great!”
Maddie Dew, reviewer

The Monty Hall problem

Australian Curriculum investigation, 2024

  • Started from an existing Monty Hall applet whose probabilities were wrong: staying and switching came out about equal. I reworked the simulation so switching wins more often, as it should, and checked the results in a spreadsheet.
  • Redesigned the game flow: Stay and Switch appear only after a door is picked, a New game button appears after each decision, and a Reset button clears the win counters.
  • Added it to the Australian Curriculum, Victorian and NSW lessons.
“I tried the simulations again and they worked a bit better!… The applet does show that you win more when switching.”
Sarah Butterworth, curriculum writer

Skills

What I do in GeoGebra

Dynamic constructions

Draggable points, sliders, checkboxes and dropdowns that let students build and test a maths idea themselves, from 3D solids to triangle congruence.

Randomisation and game mechanics

New rounds on demand, rules that always give clean numbers, instant feedback and reveals students earn by showing they understand.

Simulations

Probability simulations with animation, counters and reset, so students can run hundreds of trials and compare results with theory.

Design system

Consistent sizes, colours, fonts and LaTeX labels, locked components and colourblind-safe palettes, written up as a style guide for the whole team.

QA and debugging

Design QA against the style guide, fixing broken logic and edge cases, and checking that each applet matches its lesson’s instructions and questions.

Assets and AI

Image assets drawn in Figma. All my GeoGebra work was built by hand, without AI tools.
