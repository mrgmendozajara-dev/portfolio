Interactive maths with GeoGebra

I led GeoGebra development for Mathspace’s first-edition Australian textbooks: I created the applet design system, trained the builders and checked every applet across 12 Years 7–10 textbooks. I’ve contributed to about 450 applets and investigations, from simulations and games to step-by-step explorations.

Comparing right angles

Students drag the orange angle to any size, then decide whether it’s bigger or smaller than the right angle beside it. “New angle” gives fresh practice, and “Show angle measure” lets them check their reasoning.
